CASE BRAIN ATTORNEY NOTES OVERRIDE

Santiago now sends these fields to the existing Case Brain webhook:

body.attorneyCaseNotes
body.intakeSource
body.preview.originalMondaySummary
body.preview.notesPreview

IMPORTANT:
body.preview.notesPreview is already replaced by the attorney notes when the attorney
entered anything in the optional Santiago field.

For maximum safety, wherever your Case Brain prompt currently chooses the intake
summary / case notes, use this priority:

const attorneyCaseNotes = String(body.attorneyCaseNotes || "").trim();
const mondaySummary = String(
  body.preview?.originalMondaySummary ||
  body.preview?.notesPreview ||
  ""
).trim();

const primaryIntakeNarrative =
  attorneyCaseNotes ||
  String(body.preview?.notesPreview || "").trim() ||
  mondaySummary;

const primaryIntakeSource =
  attorneyCaseNotes
    ? "Attorney-supplied initial call notes"
    : "Monday Short Case Summary";

Then pass both into Case Brain:

PRIMARY INTAKE SOURCE:
\${primaryIntakeSource}

PRIMARY INTAKE NARRATIVE:
\${primaryIntakeNarrative}

OPTIONAL ORIGINAL MONDAY SUMMARY:
\${mondaySummary}

RULES:
- If attorneyCaseNotes is non-empty, treat those notes as the primary intake narrative.
- Do not silently merge a conflicting Monday summary into the attorney notes as though both are confirmed.
- The original Monday summary may still be used as a secondary source for comparison and contradiction detection.
- If the attorney notes and Monday summary conflict, preserve and flag the contradiction.
- If attorneyCaseNotes is blank, preserve the existing behavior and use the Monday Short Case Summary.
